Our Chania — Our Recommendations

We live here, and these are some of the places we love.

For the perfect coffee:

Mikro cafe, Little Monkey, The Jar, Koukouvagia

For dinner:

Koum Kapi Area:
Aktaion, Fourogatos, Pita Goal (Greek Souvlaki), Chrisostomos, To Stachi (Vegetarian)

Old Town Area:
Kavouras, Kariatis, Arismari, Tamam, Kouzina epe, Chalkina, Pallas

Other Areas:
Blue, Periplous, Kertos, Manos, Ntounias (Drakona village), Kipos Efrosinis

For a drink:

Rock Sugar, Thri, Sinagogi, Vineria

For a beach day:

Koum Kapi Beach, Elafonisi, Balos, Seitan Limani, Falasarna, Palaiochora, Preveli, Georgioupoli,

With kids:

Municipal Garden, Mikrofyies

For sunset:

Walk all the way up to Chania lighthouse at the afternoon, or watch the sunset from rosa nera promachon at the old harbor.
