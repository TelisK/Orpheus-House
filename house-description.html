Orpheus House — Your seaside escape in Chania

Imagine waking up to the sound of the sea, opening the balcony doors and watching the first light of the day unfold over the Cretan horizon.

Welcome to Orpheus House, a beautifully renovated seaside apartment in the heart of Koum Kapi, one of Chania’s most atmospheric neighbourhoods.

Set on the first floor and just across from a small sandy beach, the apartment offers a rare combination of panoramic sea views, authentic local atmosphere and easy access to the best of Chania.

The heart of the home is its bright and inviting living space, designed for slow mornings, relaxed evenings and everything in between. Large windows bring the sea into the apartment, while the private balcony becomes the perfect place to enjoy your morning coffee, a glass of wine at sunset or simply watch life unfold along the waterfront.

The apartment offers two separate sleeping areas, with a comfortable queen-size bedroom and a second sleeping area with a single bed. The living room also features a sofa bed, allowing the home to comfortably accommodate up to four guests.

A fully equipped kitchen, modern bathroom, washing machine, Wi-Fi, two air-conditioning units and a 32" Smart TV provide all the comforts you need for a carefree stay.

But what truly makes Orpheus House special is its location.

The sea is right in front of you. Koum Kapi’s cafés, restaurants and bars are only a few steps away, while the charming streets of Chania’s Old Town and the Venetian Harbour are within walking distance. You can spend the day exploring the city, return home for a quiet moment by the sea, and step out again when the neighbourhood comes alive at night.

Every detail of Orpheus House has been thoughtfully chosen to create a home that feels personal rather than ordinary. From its carefully selected décor to the handmade artistic touches, the apartment reflects the creativity and character of Crete.

Whether you are looking for a romantic seaside getaway, a relaxing family holiday or a beautiful base from which to discover Chania, Orpheus House invites you to experience the city at your own pace with the sea always just outside your door.

Imagine Your Stay

Imagine waking up slowly, opening the balcony doors and hearing the sea before you even get out of bed. Your morning coffee comes with a view of the Cretan blue. Later, you wander through the old streets of Chania, swim in the sea just steps from home, and return as the sun begins to set. In the evening, the lights of Koum Kapi come alive. You sit on the balcony, breathe in the sea air and realise you have nowhere else you need to be. This is Orpheus House — a little home by the sea, made for slowing down, feeling present and making memories.**
