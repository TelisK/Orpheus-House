  • Your seaside escape in Chania!

Koum Kapi is a historic, vibrant seaside neighborhood in Chania, Crete, located just outside the eastern walls of the Old Town, famous for its lively coastal promenade and urban beach.

History & Name

    • Sabbionara Gate: The area was originally named Sabbionara by the Venetians, meaning the Gate of the Sand. The historic gate still stands today.
      Turkish Influence: The name Koum Kapi comes from the Turkish Kum Kapısı, which translates to the exact same phrase, "Gate of the Sand."
      Chalikoutides Community: In the late 19th century, the neighborhood became home to the Chalikoutides, a community of African-descended workers who settled in Chania.

    • The Beach & Promenade

    • Akti Miaouli: This is the main 1-kilometer coastal pedestrian walkway, packed with cafes, meze bars, and nightspots offering unobstructed views of the Cretan Sea.
      • Koum Kapi Beach: A small, unorganized sandy and pebbled urban beach. It features public showers and is popular for a quick swim close to the city center.
