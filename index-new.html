Orpheus house is an apartment located at Koum Kapi, a district with long history, accross of a small sandy beach. It's one of the most beautiful neighborhoods of Chania, as it offers wonderful sea view and many remarkable cafes and taverns.Location is ideal, few steps from the old town of Chania and the city market and near the East Moat public parking. Enjoy your breakfast on our balcony overlooking the sea, and sleep listening to the sound of the waves. Feel like home !
