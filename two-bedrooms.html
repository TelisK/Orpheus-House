The Space

Orpheus House is a bright and thoughtfully designed apartment where comfort meets the relaxed character of a seaside home.

The apartment features an open-plan living and dining area, filled with natural light and connected to the private balcony overlooking the sea. It is a comfortable space to relax after a day in Chania, enjoy a meal, read a book or simply take in the view.

The fully equipped kitchen is part of the living area, allowing you to prepare everything from a quick breakfast to a relaxed dinner at home.

Bedrooms & Sleeping Areas

The apartment offers two separate sleeping areas, providing privacy and flexibility for couples, families or friends travelling together.

* Master bedroom: A comfortable queen-size bed, designed for restful nights.

* Second sleeping area: A separate space with a single bed, ideal for a child or an additional guest.

* Living room: A comfortable sofa bed provides additional sleeping space for up to two guests.

The apartment can accommodate up to four guests.

Bathroom & Facilities

The modern bathroom is designed with simplicity and comfort in mind and includes everything you need for your stay. A washing machine is also available in the apartment for your convenience.

For a comfortable stay throughout the year, the apartment is equipped with two air-conditioning units, reliable Wi-Fi 100 Mbps and a 32" Smart TV.

And then there is the balcony — perhaps the most special part of the home. Step outside in the morning and enjoy your coffee with the sea in front of you, or return in the evening and watch the colours of the sky change over Chania.

Everything has been arranged to make Orpheus House feel like a real home by the sea, rather than simply a place to sleep.
